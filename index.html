§ 01

Abstract

I am a Master's student at BUPT (Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications), studying at the Intelligent Computing Systems Interdisciplinary Lab (admitted via 保研 / direct recommendation).

My research investigates how structured exploration and test-time computation can unlock the latent reasoning capability of large language models — from tree-based policy optimization (TreePO) to entropy-eliciting trajectory exploration (FR3E), and from benchmark construction (SuperGPQA, 285 disciplines) to multi-agent test-time scaling on GAIA.

In industry, I've worked across ByteDance Seed, OPPO AI Agent Team, and Tencent Honor of Kings AI — translating research ideas into production reward models, data pipelines, and agent training systems.

§ 04

Publications

  1. [01]

    FR3E: First Return, Entropy-Eliciting Explore

    RLVR · structured exploration · math reasoning

    A structured exploration framework that fixes premature convergence and unstable exploration in uniform-credit-assignment RL (e.g. GRPO). FR3E walks the correct trajectory, computes per-token entropy, identifies high-uncertainty decision anchors, and launches targeted rollouts from them — yielding semantically grounded intermediate feedback without value models or dense reward labels. Significantly outperforms GRPO++ on AIME24 / Math500; entropy steadily rises instead of collapsing.

  2. [02]

    TreePO: Bridging the Gap of Policy Optimization Efficiency and Efficacy with Heuristic Tree-based Modeling

    policy optimization · tree search · efficiency

    Models sequence generation as tree-structured search. Combines dynamic tree sampling with fixed-length segment decoding, using local uncertainty to drive branch expansion. A segment-level advantage estimator mixes global and local proximal updates. With shared-prefix amortization and early-stop pruning: 22-43 % less GPU time, 40 % fewer trajectory-level and 35 % fewer token-level computations; AIME accuracy 17.13 → 27.83 % with +40 % traj/s throughput.

  3. [03]

    SuperGPQA: Scaling LLM Evaluation across 285 Graduate Disciplines

    evaluation · benchmark · long-tail knowledge

    A graduate-level benchmark spanning 285 disciplines, ≥50 questions per field, filling the long-tail evaluation gap (light industry, agriculture, services… 200+ specialties). Introduces a Human-LLM collaborative filtering pipeline mobilizing 80+ domain experts iterating with LLM responses. Even SOTA DeepSeek-R1 reaches only 61.82 % (o1: 60.24 %), confirming the benchmark's difficulty and discriminative power.

  4. [04]

    Scaling Test-time Compute for LLM Agents

    test-time scaling · agent · GAIA

    First systematic study of test-time scaling for language agents, with comprehensive ablations on GAIA (165 samples, 3 difficulty tiers). BoN dominates parallel sampling (+8 pts); score-based reflection beats step-by-step; list-wise verification/merging beats scoring & voting. Heterogeneous multi-model cooperative sampling (GPT-4.1 + Claude-3-7 + Gemini-2.5-Pro) hits pass@4 = 74.55 %, beating open-source SOTA.

  5. [05]

    Improving Food Recognition with Retrieval-Augmented and Domain-Adaptive LVLMs

    LVLM · retrieval-augmented · domain adaptation

    Two complementary remedies for LVLMs' weakness on food recognition. RAR: retrieve top-K similar-image categories, augment LVLM input — training-free; LLaVA 1.5-7B → Food101 Top-1 80.27 %. DAR: two-stage training (multi-task pre-training + food fine-tuning) → Food101 Top-1 95.30 %.
